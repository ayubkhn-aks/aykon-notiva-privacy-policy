Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Notiva mobile application (herein referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by A&K Tech (herein referred to as the "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

🛡️ Privacy First: Notiva operates entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, notes, photos, or custom canvases over the internet.

Data Collection

The Application runs locally on your device. The Service Provider does not collect, store, or share any personal information with any server or third party.

Any content you create within the Application—including text notes, sketches, drawings, and photos—is stored strictly on your device's local storage (using local secure Hive database files). None of this data is ever uploaded or transmitted to external servers.

Permissions We Use

To provide its core note-taking, sketching, and alert functions, the Application may request access to the following device features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display local reminders and custom alerts about your notes and scheduled tasks. No notification data is sent to any server.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used to schedule local reminders and alerts at precise times. This functions completely offline on your device and enables alarms to trigger reliably even when the device is in low-power idle modes.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Allows the device CPU to temporarily wake up when a scheduled reminder is triggered so the notification can be displayed immediately.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to restore your scheduled local reminders automatically after your device restarts. No data is collected or transmitted.
  • Photo Library & Camera: Used to let you select or capture photos to insert directly into your notes or custom canvases. These files remain stored locally on your device.

Third Party Services

Notiva does not use any third-party services, analytical trackers, SDKs, or advertising networks. No data is shared with, sold to, or monitored by any third party.

Data Security

All notes and settings are stored locally on your device. Since all data is stored locally, you are in full control of your data at all times. We recommend securing your device with PIN/biometric authentication to protect your local data.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect any data from anyone including children under 9. Our app is intended for users aged 9 and above. Users under the age of 9 should not use this application.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can permanently delete all your data at any time by:

  • Clearing app data / cache from your device's system settings
  • Uninstalling the Application

These actions are handled by your mobile operating system and are completely irreversible.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
